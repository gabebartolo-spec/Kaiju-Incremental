# Credits

Third-party work that needs a credit line (the game's credits screen, when it exists, copies these). The licence
status of everything in the repo is in `docs/LICENSING_REGISTER.md`.

## Icons

- HUD skill icons (breath, dash, ultimate): silhouettes "T-Rex Skull" and "Spiked Dragon Head" by Delapouite
  (https://delapouite.com), and "Power Lightning" and "Bird Claw" by Lorc (https://lorcblog.blogspot.com), from
  https://game-icons.net, licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Recoloured, combined with
  our own beam and streaks, and clipped to a disc.
