## Description
You are SPECIMEN K-01, a giant crocodile kaiju rising from the bay at dusk. Each rampage lasts 60 seconds (up to 3
minutes with timer upgrades). Flatten buildings, vehicles and the army sent to stop you, and devour the biomass of
everything you crush. Between rampages, spend that biomass on the evolution tree, then go again, bigger.

- **Destruction first:** buildings break apart floor by floor; bridges, quays and landmarks fall.
- **A living predator:** heavy, lumbering motion; a tail lash, a breath attack, an ultimate and a leap.
- **The Taskforce fights back:** soldiers, helicopters, jetpack agents, mechs and gunships. When time runs out, an
  airstrike. When you die you keep everything you earned, and the next specimen is released.
- **A campaign on a hologram globe:** conquer Ostia Minor, then Carthago or Thule.

**This demo:** the croc only; Ostia Minor plus one of Carthago or Thule; about 2 hours. The second conquest ends the
demo with your rampage stats and a feedback link, and unlocks endless mode. English only.

AI disclosure (itch asks for it; wording from docs/release/steam-ai-disclosure.md): this game contains pre-generated AI
content made during development (music, sound effects, and 3D kaiju models first designed with AI, then rigged and
edited by hand). The game uses no AI while it runs.
