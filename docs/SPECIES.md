# Species identities

Cap: 120 lines. Source: the director, 2026-10-16 (D-035). Each species plays differently, not just
looks different; every line below must change what the player sees, decides or remembers.

## Reptilian: the strongest, a living battering ram
Director: "our strongest physical creature, his tail can shear through buildings and his front on
momentum is nigh unstoppable ... ultimate power is an enormous beam of energy shooting from his mouth
(like godzilla), painting everything before him in devastating glowing death".
- **Tail sweep:** the tail is a weapon. A sweep behind and to the side shears through building chunks
  (sim: an arc hit behind the body, strong falloff-free cut that topples upper floors).
- **Momentum:** damage and knockback grow with speed held in a straight line; walking straight through
  a block barely slows him. Turning bleeds momentum (the choice: commit to a line or turn).
- **Ultimate (fully evolved): Atomic Beam.** A long, sustained beam from the mouth, swept by steering;
  everything it crosses ignites and glows (lingering burning/glowing ruins, sim fire on every chunk hit).
  The current Atomic Breath cone is the early, small version of it.

## Insectoid: the fastest, a skittering hunter
Director: "our fastest creature, it scuttles and jumps (hover mode?) and can climb the taller
buildings ... ultimate power is the ability to lay eggs which spawn elephant sized babies which run
amongst the city devouring civilians and fighting the cops/military".
- **Scuttle:** highest speed and acceleration, sharp turns, short bursts.
- **Jump / hover:** a leap (today's Leap Slam) that can chain into a short hover/glide (wing-buzz) to
  cross streets and pick a landing; landing crushes. Open question: hover as a held button or automatic.
- **Climb:** can scale tall buildings and fight from the top or the side; weight on a tower can tip it
  (sim: climbing adds load to upper chunks; topples from the top).
- **Ultimate (fully evolved): Brood.** Lays eggs that hatch elephant-sized young; they run through the
  streets eating civilians and fighting police and military on their own (sim: autonomous minions with
  simple seek/attack, capped count, seeded).

## Cephalopod: the most cunning, a hydra of limbs
Director: "our most cunning, it destroys in 360, its limbs like the heads of a hydra wreaking destruction
about itself, it can push, pull, climb, squeeze itself anywhere ... ultimate power is the ability to spray
highly corrosive ink, painting attackers and buildings alike in atom-searing gunk". And: "can pick up and
throw his attackers, wielding a tank like a grenade, while one of his other limbs plucks a helicopter from
the sky hurls it into a building".
- **360 hydra:** each tentacle acts on its own, hitting whatever is near in every direction (sim: several
  independent limb attacks per second around the body, each choosing a target).
- **Grab and throw:** limbs seize tanks, cars and helicopters (even airborne) and hurl them into buildings
  or other attackers; a thrown tank explodes like a grenade. The player aims throws; limbs pick targets.
- **Push, pull, climb, squeeze:** pulls itself along by building edges (locomotion D-031), climbs, and
  squeezes through narrow gaps between buildings other kaiju would have to smash through.
- **Ultimate (fully evolved): Corrosive Ink.** Sprays ink that coats buildings and attackers in glowing
  gunk; coated chunks dissolve over a few seconds and coated units die (sim: a damage-over-time decal).
