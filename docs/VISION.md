# Vision

Cap: 120 lines. Changed only by a director decision (`docs/DECISIONS.md`).

**One sentence.** You are a kaiju: tear through a voxel city in short, frantic rampages, spend
the rubble on a deep evolution tree, and come back bigger until no tower can stand before you.

**Pillar.** You are the disaster: every rampage must feel bigger than the last.

## What it is
- An **active action game** with incremental progression (D-002). No idle, no AFK, no offline
  gains: progress comes only from playing. Authored pacing, about **3-5 hours** to the end.
- **Short rampages** (D-003): 40 seconds to a few minutes each, ended by the clock, the army or
  the city falling. Between rampages: the evolution tree.
- **Move + auto attacks** (D-004): you steer; stomps fire on their own; one signature attack
  on a button. Keyboard and controller; readable on a TV.
- **Three species** (D-006, D-007), chosen at the start, each with its own body, stats and
  signature attack: Reptilian (brawler, Atomic Breath cone), Insectoid (fast, Leap Slam),
  Cephalopod (slow, huge reach, Tentacle Lash ring).
- **Voxel art** (D-005): the city, the kaiju, the army and the debris are voxels; buildings
  lose layers and spill blocks as you smash them.

## The core loop
Rampage -> earn rubble from every layer and tank -> buy evolution nodes -> rampage again,
reaching further. **Size tiers** gate the city: a building taller than your tier barely feels
your blows ("TOO TALL - EVOLVE"), so each EVOLVE node opens a new skyline.

## Escalation
The army spawns faster the more you destroy. Tanks now; jets, mechs and a rival kaiju are
roadmap items for the director to assign.

## What it is not
Not a clicker, not idle, not infinite exponential numbers, not a city builder.

## Open (director)
- How many cities, and what ends the game (a final boss, a world map, the last city).
- Kaiju customisation beyond species (colours, parts, mutations shown on the body).
- The look: this greybox is placeholder voxels; the art direction comes with labelled captures.
